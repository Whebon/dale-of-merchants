## Dale of Merchants on BGA

Dale of Merchants is a cute deck-building game, originally published by Snowdale Design in 2015 and remastered in 2026. 
This implementation supports 33 of the 36 animalfolk decks from the six-part Dale of Merchants series.

Play Dale of Merchants with 1 – 4 players for free on [Board Game Arena](https://boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=daleofmerchants).
